CSV Format:

Store, Region, Item, Count, Note

• Leave Item empty (e.g., Store,Region,) to create blank lines
• Just Store creates a blank line under that store
• Just Store,Region creates a blank line under that region
• Region can be left empty to list items directly under store
• Count/Note: Use true, 1, x, or yes to add blank lines (empty = false)
• All trailing commas are optional
